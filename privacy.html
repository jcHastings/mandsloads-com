Privacy Policy

Last updated August 26, 2026

M & S Loads LLC (“we”) operates mandsloads.com and the MS Express dispatch tools. This page covers the public website and the phone numbers we use to message drivers and carriers.

What we collect

  • Name, phone, email, and company when you do business with us
  • Load, appointment, and equipment details needed to haul freight
  • Message logs for SMS and WhatsApp we send or receive for dispatch
  • Basic website logs (browser, IP, pages viewed) from hosting

How we use it

We use this information to dispatch freight, keep appointments, pay and get paid, and meet safety and insurance requirements. We do not sell personal information. We do not use your number for unrelated marketing lists.

Sharing

We share what a load requires with shippers, receivers, insurers, and service providers that already handle that freight (for example messaging or GPS vendors). We share information when the law requires it.

Retention

We keep load and message records for as long as we need them for the haul, billing, claims, or the law. Ask us if you want a copy or a correction.
