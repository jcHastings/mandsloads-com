SMS and WhatsApp terms

Last updated August 26, 2026

M & S Loads LLC / MS Express uses SMS and WhatsApp to talk with drivers, owner-operators, and carriers about live freight.

What you get

  • Load offers and rate confirmations
  • Pickup and delivery appointment updates
  • Check-call and tracking requests
  • Reefer setpoint / alarm notices when they affect your load

Consent

We message numbers that belong to people who work a load with us or who asked to be on the list. Message frequency follows the freight, not a daily blast. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out

SMS: reply STOP to leave the list. Reply HELP for help. WhatsApp: tell dispatch to stop, or use WhatsApp’s block / mute controls.

Help

Email dispatch@msloads.com or jc@msloads.com.

Carriers and drivers are not charged by us for these messages. Your phone plan may bill standard SMS or data.
